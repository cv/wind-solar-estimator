    Wind turbines

    Power curve against the physical limit for the capture area below

    Left and right arrows pick a point, up and down change its output (Shift for larger steps), Delete removes it.

    The default is an illustrative 3.5 kW turbine with a 6 m rotor. Paste the real turbine's independently measured curve.

    Measure the whole device as the wind sees it, not the throat inside: a scoop's or duct's full front opening, or a 360° intake's side outline. Energy can only come from wind passing through this area.

    Leave at 1 when the area above is the device's full outline. Raise it only for a rotor inside a larger duct, and only by a measured gain.

    Site wind calibration

    Weather data averages wind over a ~28 km grid cell, so it can be well off at a specific site. If you know the site's long-term mean wind at hub height, from a nearby station or measurements, enter it and every hour is scaled to match.

    Solar panels

    Auto tilts the panels at about the latitude, facing the equator (180° is south). Changing tilt or facing downloads new sunlight data.

    Electricity use

    Battery and inverter

    A battery with only half its capacity usable has a minimum charge of 50%.

    When wind and solar feed one inverter, anything above its rating is lost. Leave empty if each has its own, or the inverter is large enough.

    Weather data

    Weather comes from the Open-Meteo historical API (ERA5 reanalysis, CC BY 4.0). The free tier is for non-commercial use; commercial use needs an API key. The key stays in this browser and is never put in shared links.

    See how wind and solar would perform at any site

    Pick a place: search for it, click the map or type its coordinates. The estimator replays every hour of the last 20 years of weather there through the turbines, the solar panels, the site's electricity use and a battery, then tells you what the turbines add. Adjust the assumptions to match the customer and the report updates as you type.

    Estimates are indicative, not a bankable energy assessment. They're only as good as the power curve and the wind data.
