Wind + Solar Site Viability Estimator Prototype

1. Location

    Or click the map. The first analysis of a site downloads its weather history (up to a minute or two); after that it is cached in this browser.

    2. Wind turbines

    Default: an illustrative 3.5 kW turbine with a 4 m rotor. Replace it with the real turbine's independently measured curve.

    3. Site wind calibration

    Reanalysis data averages wind over a ~30 km grid cell, so it often understates (or overstates) wind at a specific site. If you know the site's long-term mean wind at hub height (from a nearby station or measurements), enter it and all hours are scaled to match.

    4. Solar PV

    Auto: tilt ≈ latitude, facing the equator (180° = south). Changing tilt or facing downloads new irradiance data.

    Data source

    Weather: Open-Meteo historical API (ERA5 reanalysis, CC BY 4.0). The free tier is for non-commercial use; commercial use needs an API key. The key stays in this browser and is never put in shared links.

    How it works

    1. Pick a site (search, click the map, or type coordinates) and press Analyse this site.
    2. The tool downloads hourly wind (10 m and 100 m), temperature and sunlight for every hour of the chosen years.
    3. It simulates every hour: turbine output from the power curve, solar output, the site's electricity use and a battery, with and without the turbines.
    4. Change any assumption on the left and the results update instantly.

    Results are indicative, not a bankable energy assessment. They are only as good as the power curve and the wind data.
